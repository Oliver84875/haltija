// ABOUT US

OUR STORY | HALTIJA TACTICAL

Founded in Finland, HALTIJA TACTICAL was born from a shared vision between three founders: Oliver, Leevi, and Tomi. What began as a concept during an entrepreneurship course quickly evolved into a dedicated mission to design and deliver reliable, high-performance tactical gear.

Driven by a collective passion for the military, armed forces, and active airsoft sports, our team understands the demands placed on gear in rugged, real-world conditions. Oliver brings a focus on firearms, weapon mechanics, and fundamental safety protocols, ensuring our design philosophy emphasizes functional utility, security, and precision. Combined with Leevi and Tomi’s deep knowledge of tactical equipment, loadout configurations, and modular setups, HALTIJA TACTICAL is built on practical experience and a commitment to quality.

Inspired by Finnish nature and the spirit of the haltija—the legendary guardian spirit and protector—we engineer equipment built to withstand tough environments. Whether on the field or in training, HALTIJA TACTICAL provides modern operators and enthusiasts with gear engineered for mission success.
